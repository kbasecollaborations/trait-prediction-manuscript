The four source phenotype--genotype datasets analysed in this study are available from their original publications or compiled phenotype-matrix sources: ATLeaf~\cite{Schafer2023-nk}, Biolog phenotype data~\cite{BiologDatasetCitation}, Marine~\cite{Gralka2023-ft}, and Populus~\cite{Timm2015Populus}.
